    About

    I like understanding how things work, then building something better.

    My background is robotics, where mechanics, electronics, control and software have to work together. I enjoy the overlap: going from models and CAD to hardware, debugging communication, and checking whether a system performs as intended.

    My projects span UGV motor control over CAN, LiDAR stabilisation, electronics and PCB design, Bluetooth localisation and C++ software. I care about practical results, clear documentation and learning fast when something fails the first time.
